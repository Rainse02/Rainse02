## Research

My research focuses on the intersection of **battery science, artificial intelligence, and scientific computing**, with an emphasis on connecting physical understanding with data-driven methods.

- **Battery Intelligence**  
  Sensor reconstruction · SOC/SOH estimation · degradation and lifetime modeling · battery management systems

- **Electrochemical Diagnostics**  
  EIS · DRT · sparse-frequency reconstruction · physics-informed machine learning

- **Battery Microstructure**  
  X-ray CT · FIB-SEM · segmentation · 3D reconstruction · defect characterization

- **Generative AI for Physical Systems**  
  Diffusion models · physics-informed generation · time-series modeling · AI-assisted scientific workflows
